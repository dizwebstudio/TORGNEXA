# Russia Compliance & Regulated Integrations

Plugin families cover:
- Chestny ZNAK: marking status, National Catalog, phased SUZ/True API writes;
- UKEP Signing Service;
- MChD authority references/verification;
- EDO: Diadoc/Saby and future providers;
- KKT/OFD fiscalization interfaces;
- VetIS/Mercury;
- EGAIS GovernmentConnector;
- Product Compliance: certificates/declarations/EAC and extensible compliance evidence/policy.

Government/EDO remote systems remain authoritative for official statuses. Legally significant writes default to approval and complete audit.
